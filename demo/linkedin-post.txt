I built an AI that negotiates aircraft sales and leases. It can't set the price, and that's on purpose.

A rules engine decides every number. In round one it gives up 25% of the room between list and floor, then 45%, then 65%. Round four goes to a human. The language model only words the reply.

The floor price never reaches the model. A guardrail rejects any draft that leaks it, or that sounds like a binding offer. I didn't want an AI that bluffs.

Nothing goes to a buyer until a named broker approves it. Sanctions screening runs before a deal can even start.

Why so strict? One wrong sentence in an aircraft deal can cost real money, and I don't trust a chatbot that just does its best. I wanted one that knows exactly where it stops, plus 20 automated test suites that prove it does.

The demo runs in your browser with 11 invented aircraft. Nothing's saved or sent. Open a deal, read the draft, approve it yourself.

Which step of a deal wouldn't you hand to software?
